    WHEN TO GET HELP

    Don’t wait for the timer

    Go to your maternity unit for assessment if you think your waters have broken. Contact them immediately if you have bleeding, your baby moves less than usual, you think labour may be starting before 37 weeks, or something feels wrong. If you cannot reach them, seek care directly.

    Seek urgent help for heavy bleeding, severe constant pain, or if birth feels imminent. In Singapore, call 995 for an emergency ambulance.

    If you have a planned caesarean, a previous quick labour, a high-risk pregnancy, or a long journey to hospital, follow your own care team’s earlier advice.
